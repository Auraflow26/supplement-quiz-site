# User Personas

## 1. JChest — the optimizer

**Demographics:** Tech professional, late 20s, single, commutes from Tribeca. Works out at Equinox 6x a week, has done Hyrox at least twice. Reads self-help books and listens to podcasts on walks. A little vain.

**Goals & motivation**
- Strength training, aesthetics, and longevity
- Wants to impress others with his physique and regimen
- Chases fitness trends and likes to be good at what he does
- Always searching for the best health regimen

**Pain points**
- Keeping track of supplement intake
- Lack of results in the gym
- Stomach pains from certain supplements
- Frequent fatigue with no clear cause

**Behaviors**
- Perfectionist about his health
- Tracks macros and workouts
- Doesn't drink alcohol
- Changes his supplement routine based on his favorite fitness influencer

> "I would like my biometrics to be better than the guy next to me."

## 2. Bobby McBob — the busy professional

**Demographics:** 26, works in finance.

**Goals & motivation**
- Get supplements in one step — efficiency first
- Prioritize health and feel better at work and through the day
- Make supplements part of his daily routine

**Pain points**
- No time to research supplements, but wants them personalized
- Wants to be healthier the easiest way possible
- Long hours and a stressful job — needs the right supplements the first time, no trial and error

**Behaviors**
- Long work hours, stressful environment
- Limited free time
- Health conscious

> "I'm so stressed and feeling really unhealthy from my work hours so I need to be able to get supplements that are just for me but I don't want to do the research."

## 3. Anna — the new mom

**Demographics:** New mom caring for a newborn, recovering postpartum. Doesn't know much about supplements but wants to recover fast, be strong for her kid, and feel vitality again.

**Goals & motivation**
- Return to pre-pregnancy fitness (realistic timeline: 6–12 months)
- Have energy to keep up with the baby without exhaustion
- Rebuild core strength and pelvic floor health
- Feel confident in her body again
- Learn how to care for herself in this new stage of life

**Pain points**
- Doesn't know what to take; conflicting opinions online
- Disorganized because of the newborn's constant demands
- Constantly tired from lack of sleep
- Trouble bouncing back to her old life

**Behaviors**
- Does mini workouts when she can; tracks mood and energy in notes
- Scrolls new-mom content during night feeds
- Starting to eat healthier
- Growing interest in fitness and wellness
- Juggles childcare and recovery with little uninterrupted time
- Wants solutions that fit fragmented time blocks

> "I want to learn about how to best support my health in this new stage of life."
