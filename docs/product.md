# Product Brief

**Personalized Supplements in Drink — "Supple-MEANT for you!"**

Source: Tech Management pitch deck.

## Problem

Interested in your health, but...
- There are hundreds of supplements touted online and you don't know what to take.
- Many pills are an annoyance to take every day.
- You wish your daily routine could work in sync with your health goals instead of being overwhelmed by them.

## Product

- Personalized, supplement-fortified **coffee creamer**.
- Made to order and **customized for each user**: intake based on lifestyle, nutrition, preferred flavor, etc.
- Built as a daily habit: coffee creamer infused with supplements.
  - The creamer serves as a **liposomal base**, positioned as better than pills for **bioavailability**.
  - Optimizes the effectiveness of supplements people may already take.
- Fits an existing routine: pour the creamer into your coffee or tea.

The quiz also offers two other forms: pure liposomal drops and protein shake (see [backlog.md](backlog.md#user-story-map)).

## Litmus test

| Test | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **Timely** | Large interest in supplements, but people don't know what to take. |
| **Solvable** | Feasible product (coffee creamer) using existing ingredients. |
| **Important** | Supplements are popular; people need easier ways to fit them into their routine without much thought. |
| **Profitable** | Premium pricing, high-margin personalized product. |
| **Context-fit** | Gen Z is prioritizing health in daily life and wants customized supplements over generic ones. |
